Aim: 3,620 verified visitors in Pune (+77% on 2025) on the same ₹3.0 lakh. Commit: 2,900.

CampusFest 2027, Sat–Sun 6–7 March. A visitor is a distinct external person with an admitted, verified check-in. In 2025: 2,050 visitors against a 2,200 goal — and most people were lost after they said yes: 575 confirmed never got in, 88 stayed waitlisted, 275 abandoned the form.

Why Pune

  • S517 colleges, 3rd-most of any district, after Bengaluru Urban and Jaipur (AISHE 2023-24); 8.7 lakh urban 18–24-year-olds (Census 2011).
  • SMetro: 3 lines and a 30% student pass, meeting at Shivajinagar.
  • SEarly March avoids SPPU exams (from 23 Mar) and April heat (38°C mean max, IMD).
  • Local edge: I live here, so clubs and the venue are worked in person.

Double down

Partnerships and referrals each hold back ₹12.5k until week 4 and get it only past their scale line. They are our cheapest visitors and the most uncertain at scale, so we pay to learn early.

Deprioritise or reject

  • Creators as run: ₹337 per visitor, 60% show-up.
  • Paid tickets: within an event, payers show up only 2–4 points more (PheedLoop, 195 events).
  • A headliner: a big share of ₹3L for an unmeasured effect.
  • More cold paid reach: it was saturating in 2025.

Dependencies and risks

  • Bet 1 is a precondition: success means ~440 admissions/hour at the gate.
  • Referrals need registrants: bets 2, 4 and 5 feed bet 3.
  • Partnerships and ambassadors split the college list; one recorded source per person.
  • Police permission first: NH7 Weekender 2024 was cancelled without it.

3 · How will we know what's working?Md Misbah Ur Rahman

Attendance shows only on the day — so each bet is judged weekly on leading indicators, against lines set in advance.

Rates are judged only on cohorts at least 7 days old (95% of 2025 confirmations came within 4.1 days), and every bet keeps a holdout where it can. Cost = cost per registration ÷ the all-campaign blended cost in the same weeks.

When performance changes — decided in advance

  • Past a scale line: at week 4 partnerships and referrals get their ₹12.5k; an unearned share goes to the next bet past its line.
  • At a stop line: money moves to referrals, then partnerships, then organic — never back to cold paid reach.
  • A rate turns bad: change first, re-read a week later, stop only if it stays below.
  • Forecast below 2,900 twice running, from three weeks out: start the stretch levers early.
  • Gate dry run below 450/hour a week out: add lanes, stagger entry.
  • 2027 forecast = confirmed per channel × its 2025 show-up. On 2025, the product's simpler projection was off by −1%, −15% and +3% (1 Mar, 15 Mar, 1 Apr), so act on two readings, not one.

Weekly rhythm

  • Monday: the Growth Center flags every campaign; bet owners report against these lines.
  • Owners act within 48 hours; each action is a task with an owner and a due date.

How the dashboard and AI help

  • Dashboard: replay any date; funnel vs the plan in force; where people drop out; decisions with evidence; a budget what-if on this model.
  • AI copilot: answers only from live data, shows its steps, says when the data can't answer, and creates tasks only after you confirm.
  • Tested: metrics computed twice (2,050 values, 0 mismatches); the copilot passed 38/38 checks on 64 independent questions, about ₹0.3 an answer.
